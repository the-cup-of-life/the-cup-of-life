THE CUP OF LIFE · UPDATE 1.5
🤖 VS AI MATCHESChoose VS AI or VS PLAYER. Challenge five AI levels — Amateur, Semi-Pro, Professional, World Class and Legendary — in Draft OVR, Draft Team Matchtime and One-on-One Sim, or play BLITZ 4V4 against AI. AI squads are generated from the game player pool and never enter your club or change your saved inventory.
⚡ BLITZ 4V4 MULTIPLAYERCreate a public Blitz room or join an open Blitz room from VS PLAYER. Select one GK, DEF, MID and ATT from your club. Online Blitz uses the Firebase room flow and reduced-rate state sync.
🏆 THE BACK-TO-BACK CHAMPIONSA new featured Draft pack containing Désiré Doué (106), Khvicha Kvaratskhelia (106) and Marquinhos (106), using the supplied card artwork.
🧤 THE SAVIOURSA goalkeeper pack featuring Manuel Neuer (106), Thibaut Courtois (106) and Iker Casillas (107 · ULTRA RARE). Casillas has a 5% chance in the 100 Coins Pack and 10% in the 1,000 Coins Pack; displayed odds are per pack.
🌍 NEW LEAGUE PLAYERSTurkish Süper Lig: Orkun Kökçü (85 Gold, CM/CDM) and Aral Şimşir (78 Silver, LW/CAM). LaLiga: Lamine Yamal (93 Gold, RW/LW) and Orri Óskarsson (80 Silver, ST/CF). Serie A: Kenan Yıldız (89 Gold, LW/CAM) and Mattia Zaccagni (82 Silver, LW/LM).
🎴 FAIRER PACK OPENINGSA player can appear only once in any single pack opening. Existing 100- and 1,000-Coin pack sizes and the original featured Legend probabilities remain intact.
⚖️ BALANCED AI DRAFTINGIn Draft OVR and Draft Team Matchtime, you complete an 11-round player draft while the AI independently chooses and positions its own XI. Difficulty changes the quality of the AI's selections. In One-on-One Sim, both teams are built around your selected player's OVR.
